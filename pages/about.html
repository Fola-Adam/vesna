The Story Behind Vesna

It started with a simple problem: I was constantly being asked for recommendations on digital products. Courses to take, tools to use, ebooks to read. And every time, I would give the same handful of answers because those were the only things I actually trusted.

So I decided to build something better. Vesna is my personal curation of digital products that I have tested, loved, and genuinely believe can help you. No sponsored placements, no fake reviews — just honest recommendations from someone who actually uses these tools.

I am affiliated with my sister's Selar store, which means I can offer you access to quality digital products while supporting our small family business. Win-win, right?

What Makes Vesna Different

Honesty First

I only recommend products I would use myself. If something does not meet my standards, it does not make the list.

Quality Over Quantity

You will not find hundreds of random products here. Just a carefully curated selection of things I genuinely believe in.

Real Experiences

Every product on Vesna has been personally tested by me or someone I trust. I share the pros and cons honestly.

My Journey

At 16, I am still learning every day. But I have already discovered some incredible digital products that have helped me grow — in my studies, my side projects, and my understanding of the digital world.

Building Vesna is part of my own journey too. It is teaching me about product curation, user experience, and what it means to be accountable for recommendations you make.

I hope Vesna saves you time and helps you discover products that genuinely make a difference. That is the goal anyway.
