WHAT IT IS

Hand-thrown in the heart of the Vesna Atelier, the Artisan Teapot is a study in functional sculpture. Crafted from a unique blend of carbon-rich volcanic clay, it features a non-porous matte finish that develops a subtle patina over years of use. Each piece is fired for 48 hours at extreme temperatures to ensure thermal durability and a texture that feels like sea-worn stone.

WHO IT'S FOR

Designed for the intentional minimalist and the connoisseur of ceremony. Whether you are hosting a formal tea service or enjoying a solitary dawn brew, this vessel is for those who find beauty in the slow, the tactile, and the permanent. It is as much an heirloom as it is a kitchen essential.
